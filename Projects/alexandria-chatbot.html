What to upload

The bot answers from passages, so it works best when your documents already have clear sections. Here is what each format gives you.

BEST
Markdown (.md) or Word (.docx) with real headingsHeadings become section labels and citation targets. Use Word's Heading 1/2/3 styles, not bold text. One topic per file.
GOOD
Text-based PDFExported from Word, Google Docs or a wiki. Text is extracted per page; citations point to the page. Headings aren't recognised, so long pages are split by size.
OK
Plain text (.txt), CSVIndexed as paragraphs / rows. Fine for FAQs and lists. Add a first line with a title.
NO
Scanned PDFs, images, slides with pictures of textThere is no text to extract. Run OCR first (Adobe, macOS Preview, or ocrmypdf) and upload the result. A PDF that indexes to 0 chunks is almost always a scan.
NO
Spreadsheets (.xlsx), Google Docs linksExport the sheet as CSV, and the Doc as .docx or Markdown, then upload.

Tips that make answers better

Put the document's title on the first line. Keep sections under about 300 words; split long ones with sub-headings. Spell out acronyms once. Remove anything you wouldn't want quoted back: the bot can repeat any passage it indexes. Files over ~20 MB will be slow in the browser; split them.

Privacy

Files are parsed and indexed in this browser tab and stored in its local storage. The only network calls are to the AI provider you configured, carrying your question and the matching passages. Clearing site data removes everything.

What happens to a question

1. Your documents were split into passages of about 300 words at their headings when you uploaded them.

2. The question is matched against every passage two ways: by keywords (BM25, like a classic search engine) and, if embeddings are on, by meaning (cosine similarity between vectors). The two rankings are merged.

3. If the best match is weak, you get "I couldn't find that" and no model is called. That threshold is the slider in Advanced settings.

4. Otherwise the top passages and your question go to the model with instructions to answer only from them and cite with [n]. The cited passages are shown under the answer.

Costs

Per question, roughly 2,000 tokens in and 250 out: about $0.0003 on Gemini Flash-Lite, $0.001 on GPT-5 mini, $0.003 on Claude Haiku 4.5. Embedding a 100-page document once costs under a cent with OpenAI.
